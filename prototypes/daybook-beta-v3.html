People

The people your journal has woven you to — you wrote about them, or they wrote about you. A filled point runs both ways.

Threads

What the co-op keeps writing about. Threads gather on their own; members name and keep them.

A dashed thread is still forming — waiting for a member to say so and name it. Unthreaded writing stays unthreaded, respectably.

The Open

Everything holding a future: questions with owners, wagers with sealed terms, contests before the room. Resolution is always a member act, entered as a new entry.

Nothing here nags. Age is shown so the room can see it — not so you feel it.

Gatherings

The record of meeting: the close and the demo, what's coming and what each left behind.

Words

The co-op's language, forming in use. This is also the reader's dictionary — held up for inspection.

A word joins the lexicon by use, not decree.

Your Share

Your part, computed as a reading of the record — never a score, never comparative. Another member's share is unqueryable, not merely unshown.

What this view may measure was examined by members before it was built. If it starts to feel like a scoreboard, that is a contest worth filing.
