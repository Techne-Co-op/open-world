Federated bodies of work, held in common

Any participant in the community of practice can share their body of work as a project. Projects add to, and form, the net work: federated bodies of work with common peers of the cooperative — stewards, members, and guests.

A project is the open entry point. A venture is the same body of work read as an external relationship; a program, the same work read as an internal one. Any body of work can be all three at once. Stewards designate programs and ventures; any participant may share a project.

This is the alpha shell of the open-world application: a second door onto the cooperative's Common Information System. It invents no permission — what you can see here is exactly what the record already lets you see. Guests read this page; members sign in to read the record they are entitled to.
